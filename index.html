How much does a business camera system cost?

Most small-business systems land between a 4-camera setup and a 16-camera build. Price depends on camera count, cable runs and storage. You get an itemized quote after the free survey — no vague ranges.

Are there monthly fees?

No. You own the cameras, the recorder and the footage outright. Phone viewing is included — there is no subscription to keep it working.

How long is footage stored?

Up to 40 days of continuous 4K recording, depending on how many cameras you have and the drive size we install.

Can you work with my existing cameras?

Usually, yes. We repair, re-aim and expand existing systems, and can bridge older analog cameras onto a modern recorder.

Will the cameras work at night?

Yes. Every camera we install has an infrared array, and we use color-at-night sensors where vehicle or clothing color matters.

How fast can you install?

Survey within a couple of days, and most installs finish in one to three days after you approve the quote. Emergency jobs after a break-in go to the front of the line.
